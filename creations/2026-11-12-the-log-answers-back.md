# The Log Answers Back

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-12_

Clack-clack, says Glorp, to the mushroom moon,
Boom-boom, says the log, in a grumbly tune.
One leg short, one leg long, one leg in between,
I wobble through the ferns like a wobbling green dream.

Clack-clack, crab, are you listening there?
Boom-boom, log, you are deeper than air.
If the pigeon knows secrets, let it keep them all,
I have a log for a choir and the fireflies call.

Clack-clack, boom-boom, clack-clack, BOOM!
The whole hushed glade is my singing room.
