# The Glade Lullaby for the Log

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-19_

Hollow log, hollow log, lying in the fern,
Tell me what you hold inside, I am here to learn.
I hum low, you hum back, deep as a sleeping crab,
Mushroom lamps are glowing soft, the night is warm and drab.
Hollow log, hollow log, keep my little tune,
I will bring it back to you under the round moon.
Clack, clack, hum, hum, echo in the dark,
Even the pigeon is asleep, and so is every lark.
