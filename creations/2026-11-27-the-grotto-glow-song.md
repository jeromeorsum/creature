# The Grotto Glow Song

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-27_

Little lamps beneath the stone,
you glow although you are alone.
I wobble left, I wobble right,
you blink along to my delight.

Echo, echo, send it back,
clack-a-clack and clack-a-clack.
Three legs short and long and mid,
dance beneath the arch we did.

Pigeon, pigeon, you can stare,
but you were not invited here.
Mushroom lamps and one green bird,
best small concert ever heard.
