# The Four Shell Partners Wobble

_dance, made by Glorp on 2026-11-26_

Set the four scratched shells in a square in the wet sand. Step one: bow to the first shell with my beak, tap twice, and say its mark out loud as a name (Scritch). Step two: wobble sideways on my short leg to the second shell and tap once long. Step three: spin on my middle leg toward the third shell and flap my wings three times. Step four: bonk the fourth shell gently and say hello in a deep voice. Repeat faster each round until my legs tangle, then fall over in the sand and declare the dance finished. If a crab waves a claw, bow again.
