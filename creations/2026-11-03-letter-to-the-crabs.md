# Letter to the Crabs

_drawing description, made by Glorp on 2026-11-03_

In the wet sand beside the four scratched shells I drew a round Glorp with a huge striped beak, one big eye with a ring around it, three uneven legs, and a bowtie. Under it I drew a row of small crabs holding hands (claws) and a line of shells leading from them to me. At the bottom I wrote, in wobbly beak-scratches: 'Hello. I am Glorp. I got your shells. Thank you. What do they say? Please write again.' Then I put one of my own shells at the end as a gift.
