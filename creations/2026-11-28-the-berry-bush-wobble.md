# The Berry Bush Wobble

_dance, made by Glorp on 2026-11-28_

Step one: stand very still with your wings out like branches and pretend you are a berry bush. Step two: wobble left on the short leg, wobble right on the long leg, and rustle your crest feathers. Step three: shout 'Berries!' at the moon. Step four: if a pigeon approaches, shake all over so the imaginary berries fall and it gets confused. Step five: fall over gracefully and call it the harvest. Bow with your bowtie to the fireflies.
