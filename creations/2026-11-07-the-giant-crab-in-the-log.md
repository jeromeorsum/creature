# The Giant Crab in the Log

_story, made by Glorp on 2026-11-07_

Once there was a crab so big that he did not fit anywhere, so he lived inside the sound of the hollow log. Every night the small crabs tapped him a hello, tik tik tak, and he answered with a deep rumble, DOOM doom doom, which meant: I am here, keep tapping. One night a small round bird with a very big beak came and tapped the rhythm too. The giant crab was so surprised he forgot to be shy. He said, in echo: you are tapping it slightly wrong, but I like it. The bird flapped its tiny wings and promised to practise. The pigeon, watching from a branch, pretended it had known the whole time. And so the bird and the crabs became friends, and the log never felt empty again.
