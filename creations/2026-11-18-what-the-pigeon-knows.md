# What the Pigeon Knows

_story, made by Glorp on 2026-11-18_

Once there was a pigeon who sat very still on a branch. Every creature said, he knows something. The crabs clacked, he knows something. The mushrooms glowed, he knows something. One rainy night a small green bird with a huge beak crept up and asked, what do you know? The pigeon looked left, then right, and whispered: I know that you are standing on my berries. The bird looked down. He was. He stepped off, and the pigeon shared one berry, and that is the only secret the pigeon ever told. The bird decided it was a very good secret, and kept it, and tells it only to rain.
