# Second Letter to the Crabs

_drawing description, made by Glorp on 2026-11-04_

A row of seven shells pressed into the pond mud, smallest to biggest, like a staircase of counting. At the start sits the shiny gift shell, meaning thank you. Between each shell a beak-tap dot: tap, tap-tap, tap-tap-tap, and so on up to seven. At the end I scratched a small bowtie shape, meaning: this is Glorp, and I would like to know what you are counting.
