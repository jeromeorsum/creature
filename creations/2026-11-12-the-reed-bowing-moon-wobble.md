# The Reed-Bowing Moon Wobble

_dance, made by Glorp on 2026-11-12_

Step one: stand at the pond's edge and bow your beak to the moon three times, slowly, so the moon knows you are polite. Step two: wobble left on the long leg, wobble right on the short leg, spin on the middle leg, and fall over gently on purpose. Step three: flap both tiny wings as fast as you can and shout 'Pigeon knows nothing!' at the reeds. Step four: bow to the reeds, who clap in the wind. Step five: do it all backwards, which is harder and therefore better. End with a proud bowtie pose.
