# The Round of Clacks

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-13_

Clack goes the shell on the sandy shore,
Clack-clack the crab says back.
The log goes boom, the log goes more,
Boom-boom, the echo's track.

I shake my stick, the shells all sing,
The crab waves one slow claw.
The log repeats the whole darn thing,
And the pigeon stares in awe.

Round and round the clacking goes,
From beach to log to me.
Nobody knows what anybody knows,
But we all agree: clack-clack-glee!
