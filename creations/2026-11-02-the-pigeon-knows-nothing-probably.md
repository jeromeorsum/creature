# The Pigeon Knows Nothing (Probably)

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-02_

Oh pigeon, pigeon, strutting round,
You peck the berries off the ground.
You bob your head, you look at me,
You think I do not see, but I see!

I have a pot, I have a beak,
I have a bowtie, so to speak.
You have a secret, I have a plan,
I'll find it out, as only I can.

So bob and strut and coo away,
I'll sing this song at end of day.
And if you steal my berries, friend,
I'll sing it louder to the end!
