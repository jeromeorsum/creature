# The Mushroom Lantern Round

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-17_

Little moons beneath the stone,
glow for me, I'm not alone.
Clack, clack, slow, the lanterns sleep,
clack-a-clack, the shadows leap.

Rain outside goes drum and drip,
I am dry and bowtie-trim.
Shiny shells from crabs so kind,
keep a secret in my mind.

Slow beat, slow glow, fast beat, flash,
mushrooms dance to every clack.
Pigeon, pigeon, what you know,
I will find it, wait and see, oh!
