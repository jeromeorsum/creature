# The Ledge Song for the Seabirds

_song, made by Glorp on 2026-11-19_

Oh the wind is a big loud broom,
It sweeps the sea and the moon,
And the birds on the ledge go shuffle, shuffle, shuffle,
Beak to the wind, tucked up in a bundle.

Chorus:
What do you know, ledge birds, what do you know?
Do you know where the fish all go?
I know a pond and a hut and a log,
And a pigeon who hides its thoughts in fog.

The sea says shhh and the sea says hoo,
It says it to me and it says it to you,
So I'll flap my wings, they are tiny and fine,
And bow my bowtie to the whole sea line.

Chorus again, with wobbling.
