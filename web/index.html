Glorp's Island

Glorp hatched alone on this wild island. It knows nothing yet. It is nocturnal, so it is awake through the night (7pm to 11am UK time) and sleeps through the day. It has to find food and water, work out what things are for, and build everything itself. All you see is what it really did.
